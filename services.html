Services — Deep & Transformational

I offer two core services designed to bring clarity, healing, and purposeful action. Each session is tailored to your needs and can be done online or in-person.

Akashic Record Reading

A soul-centered reading that accesses the energetic archive of your soul — past, present, and potential future. This is where patterns are revealed, karmic or emotional wounds are seen, and guidance from a higher perspective is offered.

  • Understand past experiences shaping your present
  • Heal emotional & karmic wounds
  • Gain clarity on relationships, career, health & life path
  • Step into your authentic power

Typical outcomes: clarity, emotional release, renewed purpose, and practical next steps inspired by soul-wisdom.

Life Coaching

Structured coaching sessions that help you set clear goals, break limiting beliefs, and design daily habits that support lasting change. My coaching blends empathy with accountability — we create a plan and then take consistent action.

  • Goal setting & vision clarity
  • Breaking limiting beliefs & confidence work
  • Practical tools for daily growth and emotional balance
  • Support through life transitions and decisions

Both services are frequently combined: we read your soul's guidance, then build practical steps to enact that wisdom in the real world.

What a Session Looks Like

Sessions begin with a brief check-in, followed by an Akashic reading or coaching conversation. Readings may be followed by practical exercises, journaling prompts, and an action plan. Sessions are held with compassion, confidentiality, and clear next steps.

Format & Logistics

Online sessions via Zoom / Google Meet; In-person sessions by appointment. Session length, pricing, and packages are available upon request — please reach out to book or ask for options.
